## Gamifying with Typing.com

Gamification is a scientifically demonstrated method of training yourself with a games based approach. When your work becomes a game, the part of your brain engaged in _play_ is activated, as well as the more formal and serious parts, embedding learning deeper, faster and with greater ease!

> [Typing.com](https://www.typing.com/) offers a comprehensive typing program featuring animated instructional videos and gamified learning. Fully customizable and aligned with ISTE, Common Core, TEKS, and other state standards. Providing more than just keyboarding skills, Typing.com offers Digital Citizenship, Creative Writing, Test Preparation Units, General Computer Skills, and Coding Units.

Over 24 years, Typing.com has served 72 million students worldwide. _Free and easy to set up and use, it is certified for accessibility!_

I'm going to suggest [a set of games involving a cute ninja](https://www.typing.com/student/game/keyboard-ninja), but you may purse [any of the games you find on the site](https://www.typing.com/student/games)

## Getting Started

Before we start playing, it's important to know how to place your fingers in the starting position for speedy, accurate typing on the [QWERTY](https://imgv2-1-f.scribdassets.com/img/document/842692231/original/380e3e9fb6/1?v=1)

> This is the standard and well tested [starting position](https://www.typingme.com/touch-typing/typing-tutor-basicposition.php) for fingers on the keyboard

#### Notice the [little bumps on the F and J keys](https://www.reddit.com/media?url=https%3A%2F%2Fexternal-preview.redd.it%2Ftil-that-the-reason-for-the-little-bumps-on-the-f-and-j-key-v0-3GG0zrW8mqf4RbHBeNWIaizmxmvXhlZmc1BoJGkH-hc.jpg%3Fauto%3Dwebp%26s%3D1e95f9af9acd4c5e8b14e7217de7976bd31b9c98)? They're there to allow your index fingers to find those positions _without looking at your hands_

And indeed, _not looking_ is what all these games are about!
