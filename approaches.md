# Writing Approaches

The only _wrong_ ways to approach writing are ones likely to bore your audience and those you are not likely ever to complete.

In this class we're concerned with escaping from our own worst judgement of our work and the preemptive _censoring_ of our written expressions.

> We want to sneak our writing past the part of our brains that says we can't do this, that we're not doing it well.

A couple of techniques come in handy here:

## Stream of Consciousness

Writing a little faster than we can really think, certainly than we can accurately _type_ is one way to fool the inner censor into letting us get more words onto the screen.

Typing from a prompt for a short period of time, frees us up from the job of deciding, even of having a meaning we wish to share. And those freedoms can be liberating!

As well, it can help us if we think of what we're doing as.…

## Discovery Writing

Many artists know that improvisation and _play_ can lead to vibrant, flowing or surprising work. Musicians especially collaborate on the fly, make things up as they go along and work out _afterward_ why something worked.

But the same approach enlivens drawing and painting and our writing can also be an act of discovery:

> It's important to know that you don't have to start out with "something to say"
